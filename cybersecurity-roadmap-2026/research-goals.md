# Research Goals

## Cyber threat intelligence
- learn how to connect signals, infrastructure, TTPs, and campaigns
- improve source triage and confidence scoring

## OSINT
- build repeatable collection and enrichment workflows
- improve signal-to-noise handling

## Detection engineering
- write clearer detections with explicit evidence and triage value
- improve false-positive awareness

## AI safety
- study misuse categories and adversarial prompting patterns
- build simple triage systems for unsafe prompts
