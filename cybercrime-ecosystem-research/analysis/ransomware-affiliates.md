# Ransomware Affiliates Ecosystem

## Executive summary
Ransomware affiliates operate as a distributed criminal ecosystem that relies on initial access, payload deployment, extortion pressure, and infrastructure rotation. This ecosystem is attractive to analysts because it reveals clear links between intrusion methods, monetization, and operational support.

## Ecosystem structure
- Initial access brokers sell entry into victim environments
- Affiliates deploy ransomware and handle encryption/extortion
- Operators maintain leak sites, negotiation pages, and support infrastructure
- Money laundering and cash-out paths support monetization

## Common TTPs
- Spear phishing
- Credential theft
- PowerShell execution
- Living-off-the-land techniques
- Data exfiltration before encryption

## Infrastructure patterns
- bulletproof hosting
- disposable domains
- proxy chains and VPN exit usage
- leak sites and negotiation portals

## Trends
- more double extortion
- faster infrastructure rotation
- growing use of access brokers
- increased operational security awareness
- more polished leak-site branding

## Analyst takeaways
The affiliate model increases scale and lowers the barrier to entry for ransomware operations. Tracking affiliates requires attention to both infrastructure and business process, not just malware names.
