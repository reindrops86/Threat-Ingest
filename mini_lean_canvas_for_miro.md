# Mini Lean AI Canvas

Product idea: An AI assistant that helps small medical clinics reduce missed appointments by sending reminders,
answering basic scheduling questions, and escalating complex cases to front-desk staff.

## User / Customer
- Small independent medical clinics with limited front-desk staff (assumption)
- Clinic managers responsible for reducing no-shows (assumption)

Review label: assumption

## Problem
- High rate of missed appointments due to patients forgetting or being confused about schedules (assumption)
- Front-desk staff spend too much time on routine scheduling questions (assumption)

Review label: assumption

## AI Solution / MVP
- Automated reminder messages via SMS/email for upcoming appointments
- AI chat interface that answers basic scheduling questions and routes complex queries to staff

Review label: assumption

## Success Metric
- Percentage reduction in missed appointments compared to clinic baseline (assumption on tracking ability)
- Decrease in front-desk time spent on routine scheduling calls/messages (assumption on measurability)

Review label: evidence_needed

## Biggest Assumption / Next Test
- Clinics are willing to trust an AI assistant with patient communications (assumption)
- Patients will respond to AI reminders and chats enough to meaningfully reduce no-shows (assumption)

Review label: next_test
