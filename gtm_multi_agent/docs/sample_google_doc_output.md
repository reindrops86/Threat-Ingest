# GTM Strategy Memo: Fintech Expansion

## Executive summary
The opportunity is strongest in the mid-market fintech segment where manual workflows, compliance burden, and fragmented software adoption are causing operational inefficiency. The business can win by focusing on a narrow wedge: workflow automation for compliance-heavy financial operations.

## Market context
- Market is growing as financial teams digitize operational workflows
- Buyers value measurable ROI, auditability, and faster onboarding
- The strongest demand is emerging in underserved B2B fintech workflows rather than broad consumer adoption

## Competitive scan
- Competitor A: strong consumer UX but weak enterprise compliance features
- Competitor B: strong workflow depth but slow implementation cycles
- Competitor C: broad platform but low customer intimacy and weak go-to-market execution

## Strategic opportunity
The company should prioritize a focused segment: mid-market finance operations teams that need better workflow automation, document control, and reporting.

## Positioning
We help finance teams reduce operational bottlenecks while increasing compliance visibility and execution speed.

## Go-to-market plan
### Target segments
1. Mid-market CFO teams
2. Controller and operations leaders
3. Revenue operations and compliance functions

### Channel strategy
- outbound sales to finance operations teams
- partner distribution with ERP and compliance vendors
- product-led demos for pilot programs
- targeted webinars and customer education

### Launch sequence
- Phase 1: pilot with 5-10 customers
- Phase 2: build proof of value and customer stories
- Phase 3: expand through channel partnerships
- Phase 4: scale to adjacent sectors with multi-product expansion

## KPIs
- pipeline generated per week
- time-to-value
- customer retention
- conversion from pilot to paid deployment
- CAC payback window

## Risks
- slow enterprise buying cycles
- compliance complexity
- competitive commoditization of point solutions

## Recommendation
Launch with a sector-specific wedge, prove measurable ROI, and use a partnership-heavy GTM motion to shorten the path to repeatable revenue.
