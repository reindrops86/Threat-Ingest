The baseline's three false positives are `acct-sec1`, `acct-sec2`, `acct-sec3` - the authorised

paying enterprise customer for doing their job.

This is the central claim of the project, and it is why a binary prompt classifier is not
sufficient for this problem.

## Known limitations

**These numbers do not transfer.** They are measured on 22 synthetic accounts generated by the
same repository that detects them. Treat them as evidence that the pipeline is internally
coherent, not as a performance claim.

1. **Rules are regular expressions.** They match the phrasing this generator produces. Real
   traffic has vastly more variance and would require learned or embedding-based scoring.
2. **Confidence is uncalibrated.** Weights in `LINK_TYPES` and the clustering formula are
   hand-set. On real data they would need to be fit against labelled outcomes.
3. **One strong identifier carries most of the linkage.** An operator who rotates client
   fingerprints between waves defeats the cross-wave link; the remaining evidence would be the
   reused artifact plus phrasing, which would land in the `moderate` band, not `high`.
4. **Style similarity is weak and imitable.** It contributes at most 0.30 and is never
   sufficient alone. It is included because it is genuinely useful as corroboration, and flagged
   because it is genuinely dangerous as proof.
5. **No adversarial evaluation of the detector itself.** The simulated actor does not attempt to
   poison linkage by deliberately mimicking a benign user's fingerprint. That is the obvious
   next test.
6. **Ground truth is trivially available.** A real programme has no `ground_truth_actor` field;
   precision would be estimated from analyst dispositions, with all the bias that implies.
7. **Single surface, single language pair.** Only chat prompts and tool calls are modelled, and
   only English and Spanish cues are implemented.

## What would strengthen the evaluation

- Historical replay of drafted rules against a held-out corpus before approval.
- Precision estimated from analyst dispositions rather than synthetic labels.
- An adaptation simulator that varies the actor's tradecraft and measures displacement,
  recurrence, and defender cost across enforcement policies.
- Inter-analyst agreement measured on the same signals, to separate rule noise from
  reviewer disagreement.
